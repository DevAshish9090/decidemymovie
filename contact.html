Before you report a recommendation problem

Two things are worth checking, because they explain most odd results:

  • Streaming availability is regional. We show what is available in your country. A film streaming elsewhere may genuinely not be available where you are.
  • Availability data can lag. Titles leave services constantly and the underlying data takes time to catch up. If “Watch now” leads nowhere, that is still worth reporting.

We read everything. We cannot always reply to every message, but anything describing a genuine fault will get a response.
